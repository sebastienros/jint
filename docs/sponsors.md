## Thank you

Thank you for sponsoring Jint, and to the individual contributors who donate their time,
expertise, testing, and feedback. Together, they help its maintainers invest in specification conformance,
performance, releases, documentation, and long-term maintenance.

If Jint saves your team time or powers a product your users depend on, please consider sponsoring its
maintainers. Recurring support makes sustained open-source work possible:
[sponsor @lahma](https://github.com/sponsors/lahma) or
[sponsor @sebastienros](https://github.com/sponsors/sebastienros), but really prefer @lahma because he deserves
it more.

## Add, update, or remove a listing

Open a [sponsor listing issue](https://github.com/sebastienros/jint/issues/new?template=03_sponsor_listing.yml)
to request a change. Include:

- whether the listing should be added, updated, or removed;
- the organization or product name and official website;
- a short description of how it supports Jint;
- an official SVG or PNG logo and confirmation that Jint may display it.

Requests should come from an authorized representative or include public evidence supporting the change.
Maintainers may update or remove listings that are outdated, inaccurate, or no longer connected to Jint.

Sponsor names and logos remain the property of their respective owners. A listing identifies project support and
does not imply endorsement of every product or service offered by that sponsor.
